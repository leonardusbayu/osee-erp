## Delivered Director controls and evidence

The module is available at `/director/` and through **Modul → Direktur**. Owner/director/finance accounts can prepare planning records; owner/director accounts approve budgets and decisions. Finance can review proposals before approval. Approval by a proposal maker or reviewer requires an explicit owner exception. Marketing can create, correct, void, and import company marketing observations, but cannot open financial records, private Director pages, exports, or chat. Auditors have Director read/export access only. The tax-reviewer role does not grant access to this module. Director conversations are restricted to their creator within the selected organization.

Planning mutations use scoped services, active membership checks, Decimal amounts, validation, transactions, audit events, and stale-version checks where records are edited. Budget consumption equals active incurred costs plus remaining commitments plus reservations; recorded payments are displayed separately. Entry replacements void the old record and create its successor atomically. Cash-line and marketing corrections also retain their original records. These workflows create no financial journal, bank transaction, tax obligation, payment instruction, or advertising action.

The current real-source archive supplies nine monthly recap pages, 6,520 printed participant occurrences versus 6,521 detail occurrences, and fourteen review exceptions. September is partial and October–December are absent. The Director views preserve those limitations and links to the archived evidence; the annual source summary is not a complete year. Printed source prices and partner labels do not become invoice price books or a canonical partner directory. Original year conflicts, merged-date ambiguity, and control discrepancies still require human review. See the [2026 recap analysis](research/2026-recap-analysis.md).

Posted revenue/expense facts are read only from the selected organization's posted journals. They remain unavailable when no corresponding postings exist, and a recorded profit difference is not a completeness certification. Imported recaps, marketing conversion claims, and assumed cash-plan balances are not substituted for posted revenue, verified bank balances, or tax turnover. The verified-bank-balance fact remains unavailable in this release. Data-readiness indicators describe evidence and gaps; they do not unlock a certified free-cash figure or authorize spending.

The cash calculator projects only active planned lines within its horizon, from the stated assumed opening balance. Users record unknown and later obligations separately. Company-wide verified liquidity, automatic commitment-to-bill reconciliation, recurring cash-rollforward controls, live channel collection, customer/order attribution, enforceable policy distribution, and automated execution remain outside this delivered slice. The [Director architecture](DIRECTOR-MODULE-ARCHITECTURE.md) describes those broader capabilities.

## Tax decisions deliberately left pending

The company is an ordinary PT and currently non-PKP; reported turnover is below Rp4.8 billion. Final-tax eligibility still depends on documented registration/facility history and applicable transition rules. No rate is activated from the turnover statement alone. See the [tax research](research/tax-research.md) for sources and effective-date caveats.

Owner/finance users can supply registration and VAT-status evidence. A designated, qualified Indonesian tax reviewer must determine and approve company rules. Current status cannot establish historical treatment automatically. The software distinguishes outgoing withholding, customer-withheld credits, own tax, commercial bill amounts, and official payment/filing evidence.

The three-role team UI does not grant reviewer authority to Owner/Direktur or Finance. Legacy reviewer memberships can still use the existing backend approval services, but provisioning qualified review and the end-user approval workflow are not delivered. Removing legacy roles from new-account choices does not mark a tax profile, obligation, or closing checklist approved.

Tax candidate extraction and draft exports do not submit anything to DJP. Annual readiness is intentionally not marked complete from a full set of bookkeeping figures alone. Official Coretax outputs, verified holiday/exception calendars, fiscal reconciliation, and filing/payment integrations remain release gates.

## Known workflow limits

- **Pre-invoice advances:** money received before an invoice remains an unmatched bank item. There is no complete customer-advance allocation, transfer, or refund workflow yet.
- **Partial courses/services:** delivery is a whole-invoice action. Session-level or percentage-based revenue recognition and partial cancellations/refunds require further design and implementation.
- **Positive withholding settlement:** a supplier bill with positive reviewed withholding cannot use the simple gross-payment matching flow. Withholding liability posting, net settlement, remittance, and official evidence must be completed together.
- **Opening balances:** nonzero bank opening balances are blocked. Historical migration needs reviewed opening journals, subledger reconciliation, and evidence; importing a bank file does not reconstruct that history.
- **Tax profile history:** references and effective dates provide initial gates, not a complete versioned archive of every historical company tax position.
- **SDM and payroll (planned):** Finance will handle these responsibilities, with payroll approval proposed for Owner/Direktur. Employee and salary data are intended to be restricted to Finance and Owner/Direktur, excluding Marketing; there is no separate HR login role. The module, payroll processing, and its specific access controls are not implemented.
- **Accounting coverage:** depreciation/fixed assets, full fiscal adjustments, complex refunds/credit notes, foreign-currency accounting, and consolidated entities are outside the delivered scope.
- **Bank automation:** files are imported manually using the supplied format. A BNI account label or imported BNI-origin statement is not a live bank connection.
- **Tax automation:** PPh 23 and annual preparation have useful starting workflows, but official report generation/submission and a fully reviewed tax calculation engine are not finished.
- **Director planning:** an approved envelope is not cash certification, and a completed decision is a manual status record. There is no automatic payment, ad activation, price update, tax action, or enforced company policy engine.
- **Marketing integration:** input is manual or through the application's CSV template. Platform conversion claims can overlap; the system does not claim deduplicated revenue, CAC, or ROAS from those observations.

## Tax chat configuration and acceptance

The optional OpenRouter adapter requires a backend key, a pinned approved model/provider pair, and positive reviewed budget/maximum-request-cost settings. It uses a fixed generic topic and approved public summaries. It does not transmit the user's original question, company records, documents, or chat history. Changing `OPENROUTER_PRIVATE_CONTEXT_ENABLED` does not enable an unimplemented private-data path.

Routing enforces the configured provider, `data_collection: deny`, `zdr: true`, parameter support, and no routing fallback. No arbitrary model tools or external URLs are executed. Local limits, budget reservations, response-size limits, timeouts, structured-output validation, source-ID checks, and stored-guidance fallback are present. A valid response schema or citation is not proof that AI prose is legally correct. Real endpoint capability, privacy terms, pricing, budget behavior, and Indonesian answer quality still require deployment approval and evaluation.

General-source seeding is idempotent and does not overwrite revoked entries or activate a company's tax regime. Guidance review freshness is enforced for chat. Automated discovery of new tax regulations and a complete review/publication workflow are not implemented.

## Director adviser configuration and acceptance

The default **Ringkasan dari data aplikasi** is a deterministic local briefing. It uses organization-scoped source facts, available book figures, approved budget positions, reported marketing spend, and persisted scenario results. It labels their different bases and retains links to local evidence. It is not presented as an external model answer.

Director external inference has its own settings: `DIRECTOR_OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, `DIRECTOR_OPENROUTER_MODEL`, `DIRECTOR_OPENROUTER_PROVIDER`, `DIRECTOR_AI_PRIVATE_AGGREGATES_ENABLED`, `DIRECTOR_AI_ENDPOINT_APPROVED`, `DIRECTOR_AI_MAX_REQUEST_COST_USD`, `DIRECTOR_AI_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD`, and `DIRECTOR_AI_USER_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD`. The [environment example](../.env.example) leaves credentials blank and gates/budgets at zero. Tax chat's `OPENROUTER_*` settings do not enable this adapter.

External dispatch also requires a current, active owner's in-app disclosure approval with a company monthly budget and request cap. Membership, disclosure version, and policy version are checked before dispatch. The request ceiling is reserved against shared application, organization, and user monthly counters. Conservative reservations remain after successful or uncertain calls; uncertain dispatches are not retried automatically. Reported provider overages disable that organization's external policy. Request/response limits, throttling, fixed model/provider routing, disabled redirects/fallback routing, structured finding/fact validation, and local-briefing fallback are implemented.

The outbound contract contains locally classified intent, permitted aggregate facts with their basis, and application-defined findings/options. The model can rank admissible codes; application code supplies all displayed amounts and prose. The original question, chat history, company/partner names, bank details, participants, raw documents, and arbitrary notes remain local. These limited aggregates are still confidential, so both deployment approval and owner disclosure are required. The adviser cannot change budgets, prices, financial records, ads, messages, or tax states.

The local policy and quota tests do not prove provider billing, endpoint capability/privacy, or real model quality. No live Director OpenRouter inference was exercised. Production endpoint terms, price ceilings, provider-side key limits, response quality, and PostgreSQL concurrency need separate acceptance. The optional external component is a constrained prioritizer, not the full advisory and execution system described in the target architecture.
